How to play

Choose a marked square. Capture White discs by enclosing them between your new disc and another Black disc. White replies automatically. Pass only when no placement is available.

What makes this a playable proof?

The game follows the strategy witness in FourSolved.px. Praxis checks the witness, extracts its Surface computation to Haskell, and GHC compiles it to WebAssembly. Every move is processed locally in your browser.

The certificate guarantees White a tournament score of at least 9 out of 16. The counters above show actual discs; tournament scoring awards unused squares to the winner.
